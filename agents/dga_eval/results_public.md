# Public DGA benchmark

Generated by `public_bench.py`. Data: Netlab 360 DGA feed via chrmor/DGA_domains_dataset (25 families) and the Tranco top-1M list V354N; 200,000 normal test names never used in training. Caught = share of DGA names scored above the threshold that flags 1% (or 0.1%) of the normal test names.

Read with care: one seed, one dataset, and the LSTM is a reimplementation trained for 6 epochs without tuning (the n-gram regularisation was chosen on a validation slice). These are not the DRIFT numbers in the README, and the shipped models were not retrained here. Dictionary-word families (matsnu, suppobox, gozi, nymaim) stay mostly uncaught by every model: their names are made of real words.
